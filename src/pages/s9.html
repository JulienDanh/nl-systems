Three inputs for wide defending

Weak own range + Weak opponent range + Small bet → defend very wide.

Check-raise criteria (properties)

  • Direct equity vs opponent's top pair (gut shot, overcard)
  • Backdoor straight draw potential
  • Backdoor flush draw (3-to-flush)
  • Opponent missed the board frequently
  • Opponent bet small
  • High card of suit > low card (blocks linear RFI more effectively)

Hand Examples

Pure CR
T52 rainbow · J♠6♠ (vs CO, ½ pot)

System: BDFD + BD straight + overcard to T → pure check-raise.

Solver: Pure CR. Agrees.

Pure call/raise
T72 (2 diamonds) · Q♦9 (vs BTN, 30%)

System: Q♦ > 9♦; overcard + BD flush → pure play. Student folded — mistake.

Solver: Pure raise or call. Q♦ (high card diamond) better.

Mix
K8x · K8s (vs HJ, ½ pot)

System: K8 = mix. K7 close, K6 pure fold.

Solver: Confirms K8 mix, K7 close, K6 pure fold.

Super gut shot
JT8 two-tone · 9♦7 (gut shot + BDFD)

System: "Super gut shot" → call. Student folded — mistake.

Solver: 9-7 offsuit pure fold, but 9♦7 is played (BDFD rescues it).
