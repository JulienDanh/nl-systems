System 6 — Check-Raising Top Pair (Short Stacks)

BB defender CR top pair on flop. Focus on short stacks (≤35bb).

Inflection: 35bb. Above → nuts-oriented CR (sets, two pair, TPTK mix). At/below → aggressive top-pair CR. Shorter = more CR.

The pattern

Top pair = CR; two pair/sets = trap (check-call) at short stacks. SPR short enough to shove river without raising.

CR hierarchy (high boards)

On Q-high (Q73), kicker determines CR frequency:

HandCR frequency
KQ, QJ, QTPure CR
Q9Heavy CR (offsuit); backdoor FD → check-call
Q8Medium mix
Q7, Q6, Q5, Q4Tapering mix
Q2Pure call
Low boards (7-high, 6-high): kicker matters less (opponent can't make top pair with low suited connectors). Draw factors matter more. More chaotic.

Sizing

CR to small size (~3x the c-bet). Short stacks = 2-street game. No need for large CR sizes.

Hand Examples

Pure CR
Q73 two-tone · KQ/QJ/QT/Q9 · 25bb

System: All top pairs KQ–Q9 → pure CR. Q8 heavy mix.

Solver: Pure CR KQ–Q9. Clean taper to Q4, Q2 pure call. Student check-called — mistake.

Backdoor FD → call
Q75 two-tone · Q9♥ · ~20bb

System: Q9o → CR. Q9♥ (BDFD) → check-call (realize flush draw).

Solver: QJ/QT pure CR; Q9 heavy (non-heart); Q9♥ → more call.

Pure CR (very short)
K84 · K7o · 13bb

System: Too short to call → CR. KQ pure CR, K7–K2 pure CR (offsuit).

Solver: KQ pure CR; K10/K9 mix; K7–K2 pure CR (offsuit). K2♦/K3♦ with BDFD → call. K8/84/44 → trap.

Two pair → trap
J96 · QJs / J9 (two pair) · 25bb

System: QJ (top pair) → pure CR. J9/J2/J3 (two pair) → trap (check-call).

Solver: QJ/JT pure CR; J9/92 → check-call; J3 pure call; J4 high-frequency call.
