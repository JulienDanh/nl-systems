System 3 — BB vs SB Limp Stab · Defending Flops

SB limps, BB checks, SB stabs. We defend from BB. Highest-impact scenario — BB win rate determines winner/loser.

Why BB matters: Skilled player goes from −112 bb/100 (walk-away) to −20 — 92 points of opportunity. UTG only has 27. BB skill is definitively most important.

The System

Step 1 — Fold target

SB bets 1bb into ~3.7bb. Risk/reward = 1/4 ≈ 25% fold. Defend ~75%. Focus on the 25% you fold.

Step 2 — Strategically relevant cards

Ace and Deuce are NOT strategically relevant. Everything has overcards to deuce / undercards to ace. No over-unders to either. Ignore them. Build strategy around other board cards.

Step 3 — Three defending mechanisms

MechanismExample (K77)
High-card defendingAll A-high pure, Q-high ~pure, J-high starts folding
Three to a straight98o, 86o, 65o — pure calls
Backdoor flush draw85 with spade pure; 85 no spade folds. High card of suit > low card.

Step 4 — Classify around the key card

CategoryDefinitionPlayability
Double oversBoth above key cardEasy play (high key card = rare; low = may fold)
Over-underOne over, one underSensitive — worst hands. BDFD often needed.
Double undersBoth belowOften fold — unless gut shots / 3-straight

Key Skills & Exceptions

Identify the worst hand on the board

On A42 → worst contain a 7 (97, T7). On K62 → worst are over-unders to 6 (73, 83). On KQ8 → worst are double-unders to 8 (65, 54, 53).

BDFD is not always enough. If you have one of the worst hands, even BDFD won't save it. 42 with spade on K77 still folds. Identify worst first, then check if BDFD rescues.

Heuristic: key card height

  • High key card (8): double-overs rare/valuable → rarely fold.
  • Low key card (5): double-overs common → fold more (plenty below).

Paired boards

Paired card unusable (can't have equity vs trips). Evaluate around unpaired high card. High-card defending dominates.

Preflop asymmetry

SB has a folding range; BB does not. 2x/3x favor BB. BB checking = capped (no AK/AQ/overpairs). SB has advantage on Broadway boards; BB on low boards.

Hand Examples

Fold
K77 two-tone · 63o

System: Paired → evaluate king. 63o: no high card, no 3-straight, no BDFD. Pure fold.

Solver: ~25% fold. A-high pure, Q-high ~pure, J-high starts folding. 98/86/65 (3-straight) pure. BDFD plays but worst (42s, 52s) fold.

Defend
A42 two-tone · J9♥

System: Ignore ace/deuce. Key=4. Gutshots (3x,5x) pure. Worst contain 7 (97,T7). J9♥ high enough → pure play.

Solver: 3x/5x pure calls. 97/T7 pure folds. 6 outcompetes 7. J9♥ pure confirmed.

Fold
K62 two-tone · Q3 (no spade)

System: Key=6. Double-unders to 6 have gutshots (43,54,53) → pure calls. Folds from over-unders (73,83,93). Q3 no spade = fold.

Solver: Dense fold in over-unders. 73 with spade still folds. 75 (3-straight) pure. BDFDs pure on two-tone.

Defend
KQ8 rainbow · 76o

System: Key=8 (high). Double-unders fold — but 76 has 3-straight → pure play. 97 (over-under, 3-straight) pure. Double-overs (T9,J9,JT) pure.

Solver: Double-unders to 8 heavily folded. 76 pure. All 3-to-flush playable on rainbow (premium). Double-overs to 8 pure.

Defend
Q83 two-tone · K4♦

System: Key=8. Worst contain deuce. K4 with K♦ = pure play (~40 bb/100). Don't fold.

Solver: 72 even with diamond unplayable. K4 with K♦ ~40 bb/100. "Treacherous fold" by a pro.
